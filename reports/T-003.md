### What changed
- Colour swatches ignore taps while the card slides, like Confirm already did (swatch click handler in `renderPicker()` checks `sliding`).
- The AI's reply never lands in a cell that is still popping in. The AI now picks its move when its turn starts; the move waits for the longer of its 400–700 ms thinking time and the end of the chosen cell's pop-in (`scheduleAIMove()`, new `cellSettledAt()`, `boardLaunchedAt`). The pop-in timings are shared constants (`CELL_POP_START`, `CELL_POP_STEP`, `CELL_POP_MS`, `CELL_POP_SLACK`), also used by `buildBoard()`.
- End-of-game steps run on one handle, `endTimer`, which `launchBoard()` clears like `aiTimer`. The win path chains confetti (300 ms, human wins only), then the action row (+800 ms, same 1100 ms total); the draw path is the row at 250 ms.
- On phones (≤ 440 px) the colour wheel's tooltip is anchored to the wheel's right edge instead of centred, so the card's sideways clipping can't cut it off. Desktop is unchanged.

### Decisions I made
- The AI now chooses its move when its turn starts instead of when its timer fires. The board is locked in between, so the result is the same; it's what lets the delay depend on the chosen cell.
- The tooltip change applies only on screens ≤ 440 px, where the card clips; desktop keeps the centred tooltip.
